Our Story

Cooked the Way It Should Be

Seven Wonders Restaurant & Bakery is a Haitian kitchen on University Boulevard. We bake patties fresh every morning, marinate the griot overnight, and cook the platters in small batches through the day instead of holding them under a lamp. Nothing here is quick, and that is the point.
